About Dermify

Hi, I'm Warisha Shahid, a Software Engineering student passionate about AI, web development, and technology. Dermify is an educational skincare recommendation project developed to demonstrate Java OOP concepts, frontend development, and problem-solving skills.

Educational purpose

This project exists to practise web development, object-oriented programming, and basic data structures and algorithms.

Dermify does not diagnose skin conditions or give medical advice. Recommendations are educational and not medical advice. For skin concerns, please talk to a qualified professional.

How the Skin Finder works

1
You choose one or more concerns, like Acne or Dry Skin.
2
Every product has tags. We score each product by how many chosen concerns match its tags.
3
Matching products are sorted (best score first, then lower price) and the rule used is explained.
